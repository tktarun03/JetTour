# Changelog

Newest first. Matches `RELEASES` in `js/core/releases.js`.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-11-08

- Refresh button (and pull down from the top) reloads your data and checks for a new version
- Pinch and double-tap zoom are off; a new extra-large text size (A++) makes everything bigger instead
- Search jets by maker or model above the class filters
- Compare tray above the tab bar while jets wait to be compared
- Touch and hold a jet card for quick actions; swipe the jet picture to move between aircraft; double-tap it to save
- Smoother motion: zoom transition into each jet, tab bar shrinks as you scroll, saved heart pops
- Erasing data now asks in an action sheet, with Back up first

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-08

- New look: thin type, frosted glass panels and dusty-blue feature cards
- Jets drawn in a three-quarter view on the fleet cards
- Aircraft, Interior, Seats and Specs tabs on every jet
- Interior shows a cutaway of the chosen section, with + markers to jump between sections
- Floorplans run nose-to-tail across the screen; seat types shown as cards
- Wider two-column layout on tablets and computers

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-07

- Twelve real business jets, light to ultra-long-range, with published specs and sources
- Aircraft overview, seat-by-seat floorplan picker and interior sections
- Compare up to three jets side by side, in imperial or metric units
- Save favourites, pick a preferred seat, Undo on every change
- Light, dark and system theme, text size, backup and restore, self-check
